01 / Introduction

Kushagra Innovations ("we", "us", or "our") operates the SlidePrint mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how SlidePrint handles information when you use our App.

By using SlidePrint, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

02 / Information We Collect

SlidePrint is designed to work primarily with files and information stored on your device.

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Contacts
  • Precise location
  • Account credentials

SlidePrint does not have a developer-operated server or cloud system for uploading or storing your PDF documents.

03 / PDF Files and Document Processing

SlidePrint allows you to select PDF files from your device using Android's Storage Access Framework.

When you select a PDF, the App processes the file locally on your device for features such as viewing, preparing, and printing.

Your PDF files and their contents are not uploaded to Kushagra Innovations' servers.

SlidePrint does not intentionally transmit your PDF files or their contents to our servers, cloud storage, or the developer.

The files you select remain under your control on your device and are accessed only when required for the functionality of the App.

04 / Local Data Storage

SlidePrint stores certain information locally on your device to provide and improve the App experience.

Processing History

The App may store:

  • File path or file reference
  • Processing date
  • Page count
  • Print mode
  • Other information required to display your processing history

This information is stored locally using the App's on-device database.

App Settings

SlidePrint may locally store your preferences, such as:

  • Paper size
  • Print quality
  • Page orientation
  • Other selected application settings

These settings are stored on your device and are not intentionally transmitted to our servers.

05 / Temporary Files

During PDF processing, SlidePrint may temporarily create generated PDF files in the application's cache directory.

These temporary files are used to complete the requested operation and are stored locally on your device.

Temporary files may be removed by the App or the Android operating system when they are no longer required.

06 / Internet Permission

SlidePrint uses the Android INTERNET permission.

The primary purpose of internet access is to allow the App to load and display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Internet access used for advertising does not mean that SlidePrint uploads your PDF documents to our servers.

07 / Advertising and Google AdMob

SlidePrint uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob is a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC. AdMob may collect and process certain information from your device for purposes such as delivering, measuring, and personalizing advertisements, subject to Google's applicable policies and settings.

Depending on your device, region, advertising settings, and applicable laws, information that may be processed by advertising services can include:

  • Advertising ID or similar device identifiers
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Advertising and interaction information
  • Diagnostics and performance-related information

The information collected by Google through its advertising services is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

08 / Third-Party Services

SlidePrint currently uses the following third-party service:

Google AdMob

Purpose: Displaying advertisements within the App.

SlidePrint does not currently use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or a separate third-party analytics platform for user tracking.

Third-party services may independently collect information according to their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of third-party service providers.

09 / No Account or Sign-In Required

SlidePrint does not require users to create an account or sign in to use the App.

There is currently no user registration, password system, or developer-managed user account associated with SlidePrint.

10 / Data Sharing

Kushagra Innovations does not sell or rent your personal information.

SlidePrint does not upload your PDF documents or their contents to a developer-controlled server.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob may be handled by those providers according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

11 / Data Backup

SlidePrint may be included in Android's device backup mechanisms depending on your Android device, operating-system settings, and the App's backup configuration.

Any Android backup is managed by Android/Google and is associated with your device/account environment. Kushagra Innovations does not receive or directly control your personal Google Drive backup.

You can manage Android backup settings through your device's Android/Google account settings.

12 / Data Retention and Deletion

Because SlidePrint primarily stores its application data locally on your device, you can manage or remove this information through the App or Android system settings.

You may delete your processing history using the Clear All History option available in the App, where applicable.

You may also delete locally stored application data by uninstalling SlidePrint or clearing the App's storage through Android settings.

Deleting the App or clearing its storage may remove locally stored history, settings, and other application data.

Third-party services such as Google AdMob may retain information according to their own policies and retention practices.

13 / Data Security

We take reasonable measures to design SlidePrint so that user documents and application data are processed locally where possible.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

SlidePrint does not intentionally transmit your PDF documents or their contents to Kushagra Innovations' servers.

14 / Children's Privacy

SlidePrint is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through SlidePrint.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

15 / Your Choices

You can control your use of SlidePrint by:

  • Selecting which PDF files the App can access
  • Managing Android app permissions and settings
  • Clearing application history
  • Clearing application storage
  • Uninstalling the App
  • Managing advertising and privacy settings available through your Android device and Google services

16 / Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, our practices, legal requirements, or third-party services.

When we make changes, we will update the Effective Date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy.

You are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
